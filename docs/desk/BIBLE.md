# Bible

House law for aircraftaviation.com. Byline: B. Harris. If another desk file disagrees with this one, this one wins.

## Grades

Use only CONFIRMED, LIKELY, WATCH, KILL.

- CONFIRMED — two independent public sources, or one primary document plus a matching public source. Only Proof writes this word.
- LIKELY — one solid public source, no contradiction on file. Label it LIKELY in public copy.
- WATCH — tip, single social post, or unmatched scrape. Packet only.
- KILL — wrong, unsourced, or a person-hunt. Delete it. Do not rewrite it.

A beat file that contains CONFIRMED is scrap. Tower sends it back.

## Mix

Count items, not words. About 70% airline, 30% bizav. Weekday brief: 4 airline and 1 bizav unless the sky breaks the split. Do not invent filler to hit the ratio.

## Public-figure jets

Allowed only when mainstream or trade press has already reported the movement. Do not add a tail, trip, or home field those reports omitted. A tracker shot alone is KILL.

## Live video

`STREAM_BIBLE.md` is the only board. Verify before embed. No search link. No invented clock. A scheduled show is not the homepage player.
