# Source list

Add a source here before it appears in a packet or in `data/home.json`. Name, URL, and what it is good for. “Saw it on Twitter” is not a source.

Primary pages beat aggregators: airline newsroom, airport, OEM, lessor, registry, regulator, filing. Trade press can support a LIKELY line. Social is WATCH until Proof matches it to a primary page.

Do not cite a login wall, a search URL, or a tracker shot as proof of who was aboard.
