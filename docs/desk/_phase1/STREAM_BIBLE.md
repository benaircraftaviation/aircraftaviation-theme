## How a stream gets on the board

1. Hangar or Ramp proposes a row with channel or video ID copied from the operator, never from a search page.
2. Proof watches enough to know the shelf. A Wednesday show is not 24/7. A 24/7 cam that goes dark for a week is not verified.
3. Proof writes the grade on the row. Only then may Voice mention the stream.
4. Tower may kill a row that turns into a person-hunt or a military-pad stare.
